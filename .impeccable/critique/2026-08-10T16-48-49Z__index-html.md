## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment (unanchored):** Split verdict. The *substance* is unmistakably this product — the responsibility-split hero panel ("Your organisation decides / Alzette operates"), the missing-middle problem framing, contract-first disclaimers ("defined in the applicable service agreement — not in marketing text"). But the *visual system* — warm off-white + one green accent, mono eyebrows, hairline-bordered card grids, dark section with a Python block — is the standard uniform of 2024 dev-infrastructure startups. Most damning: the one ownable asset, alzette-mark.svg (the Alzette river trace), is used NOWHERE — header logo is plain text, favicon is a generic green "a." glyph. Nothing in the render says "Luxembourg"; it says "well-behaved B2B template".

**Deterministic scan (isolated sub-agent):** Browser-injected detector reported 30 findings; CLI scan was degraded (missing parser deps, regex fallback, 2 findings — undercount). After false-positive triage: 18 of 30 are false positives (low-contrast rule reads CSS color, not SVG fill — the entire diagram cluster is noise). The 12 real findings: line-length ×6 (paragraphs rendering 93–165 chars/line — `p.faint`, capacity/security paragraphs, disclaimer, contact-line), kicker-above-heading ×8 (mono-caps eyebrow above every h2 — structural repetition across all sections), ai-color-palette ×3 (cyan syntax tokens in the code block — contextually weak, it's syntax highlighting), wide-tracking ×2 (hero-facts 0.08em, resp-caption 0.12em), low-contrast ×2 (`p.disclaimer` 4.2:1 genuinely sub-AA; `p.faint` 4.50:1 rounding boundary), all-caps-body ×1, hero-eyebrow-chip ×1, side-tab ×1 (`.closing-line`; CLI also flagged `.hosting-line`), overused-font ×1 (Inter resolves as primary font).

The detector additionally caught computed issues invisible in source: line-length and the 4.2:1 disclaimer contrast. ## Overall Impression

A sober, well-crafted page with genuinely excellent copy discipline and one great design idea (the responsibility panel) — undermined by a funnel that dead-ends on mailto, trust claims with zero on-page evidence, and a visual system that any inference API could wear. Biggest opportunity: make the page as provable as the product claims to be, and give it the one visual asset it owns.

## What's Working

1. **The responsibility-split hero panel** — turns the value proposition into a two-column object a partner can screenshot into a board deck. Best decision on the page.
2. **Contract-first copy discipline** — "not in marketing text", the no-legal-advice disclaimer, the missing-middle framing: native fiduciary risk language, zero hype adjectives.
3. **Craft details** — prefers-reduced-motion, :focus-visible, scroll-margin-top, SVG aria-label, mostly-AA palette.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The entire funnel dead-ends on `mailto:`**
- Why it matters: all nine CTAs are mailto with different subjects; on locked-down fiduciary workstations without a configured mail client every conversion path silently fails, with no inline fallback.
- Fix: one real request-access form (name, organisation, workload — 3 fields) as primary action; keep mailto secondary with the address visible inline.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

**[P1] Trust is asserted, never evidenced**
- Why it matters: a risk-averse buyer is asked to believe "guaranteed contractually" while the page shows no legal entity, no LU address/RCS, no privacy policy or imprint (arguably a legal obligation for a LU commercial site), no SLA figure, no certification status, no names. A company selling operational evidence that provides none about itself — dissonance at exactly the highest-stakes moment.
- Fix: legal footer block (entity, RCS, address, privacy link), one concrete SLA figure, a certification/status line — even "ISO 27001 in progress, Q3" beats silence.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable harden` (or `$impeccable clarify` for the evidence copy)

**[P2] The ownable brand asset is unused; visual language is interchangeable**
- Why it matters: alzette-mark.svg appears nowhere — not header, footer, or favicon. The layout vocabulary is standard dev-infra SaaS (detector confirms: 8 repeated kicker-above-h2 structures, side-tabs, Inter).
- Fix: mark in header/footer/favicon; reuse the river trace as a section-divider motif or the connecting line in the endpoint diagram.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable bolder`

**[P2] Models section promises a catalogue, shows only metadata field names**
- Why it matters: eight chips listing which fields a catalogue *would* have, but not one actual model, version, or price — reads as vaporware to a technical evaluator.
- Fix: show 2–3 illustrative catalogue entries (name, version, context, tier); trim chips to 4.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify` (+ `$impeccable distill` for the chip wall)

**[P3] Mobile/edge cosmetics + detector-level polish**
- Why it matters: hero-facts orphan separator dots on wrap; responsibility rows misalign on wrap; mobile nav ~2px from overflow at 390px; announce bar duplicates header CTA; `p.disclaimer` at 4.2:1 contrast; 6 paragraphs rendering 93–165 chars/line.
- Fix: hide ::before separator on wrapped items; shared grid row for resp-row pairs; reduce nav below 400px; constrain paragraph max-widths; bump disclaimer color.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable adapt` (+ `$impeccable polish`)
