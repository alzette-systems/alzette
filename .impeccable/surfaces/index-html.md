# Public marketing landing

## Purpose

Persuade non-technical decision-makers in Luxembourg financial services that private AI inference is a credible, locally accountable alternative to public AI tools, internal GPU operations, or a broad cloud commitment. The primary conversion is a conversation about one confidential pilot workflow.

## Thesis

Alzette is the missing middle: the client keeps control of workloads, users, policy, and models; Alzette deploys and operates the private inference environment. The page presents the finished service, not a release report or temporary provider route.

## Audience and story

Lead for asset managers, fund administrators, banks, insurers, and other regulated organisations. A reader should recognise the confidentiality and approval problem, understand the endpoint-and-dedicated-model operating model, see what governance and company visibility look like, then feel ready to define a focused pilot.

## Ownable visual world

Use the established Alzette system: warm paper, near-black ink, Luxembourg river green, restrained mono labels, ruled ledgers, and the river mark. The signature object is a two-party service charter that shows client decisions beside Alzette operations. Keep compositions flat, editorial, contract-like, and calm rather than imitating a hyperscaler console.

## First viewport

Lead with “Use AI on confidential work—without giving up control,” the private Luxembourg positioning, clear pilot and explanation actions, and the service responsibility charter. Local operation, dedicated client deployment, and regulated-workflow relevance must be legible without scrolling.

## Content boundary

The landing describes the intended private, locally operated service and qualifies contract-specific location, retention, support, service-level, model, and capacity commitments. Current PoC/provider evidence belongs in implementation documentation and authenticated portal views. Do not add customer logos, testimonials, certifications, benchmarks, catalogue claims, or numerical service commitments without evidence.

## Responsive behaviour

Preserve the client-versus-Alzette comparison at mobile widths, keep one clear reading order, reduce navigation to Product and Documentation on narrow screens, and avoid carousels, hidden business content, or horizontal scrolling.
