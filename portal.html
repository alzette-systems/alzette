How payment works

You pay Alzette, not Stripe.

  1. CheckoutWhen configured, Alzette opens a short-lived Stripe-hosted checkout session.
  2. ConfirmationThe server confirms payment through its billing integration; success URLs alone do not change endpoint readiness.
  3. ProvisioningDedicated capacity can still be allocating, deploying, or validating after payment.

Do not enter a provider credential, target URL, or secret here. Stripe account creation is not required for this customer flow.
