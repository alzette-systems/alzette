The missing middle

Confidential AI needs an approvable operating model.

Financial teams work with investment material, fund records, client documentation, policies, contracts, and personal information. The value of AI is real; the usual operating choices are difficult to approve.

Most organisations are asked to choose between:

  • prohibiting AI and leaving useful workflows untouched;
  • accepting public tools without a consistent control layer;
  • building and operating specialist infrastructure internally;
  • entering a large cloud relationship for a focused workload.

Alzette provides another path: private inference infrastructure, operated locally, behind the systems your organisation already controls.
